About Science Olympiad Division B

Science Olympiad Division B is a premier national STEM competition designed specifically for middle school students in grades six through eight. Throughout the academic year, teams of up to fifteen members prepare intensively to compete in twenty-three diverse events that challenge their academic knowledge, teamwork, and practical problem-solving skills. These rigorous events span a wide array of core disciplines, including life science, earth and space science, physical science, chemistry, technology, engineering, and scientific inquiry.
